Patient Inquiries

Common Questions & Clear Answers

Everything you need to know about preparing for your visit, billing transparency, and rescheduling options.

Please bring a valid photo identification card, your current prescription list (including over-the-counter supplements), and any recent laboratory work or imaging discs completed within the past 12 months.
We accept major PPO, EPO, and Medicare Advantage plans including BlueCross, Aetna, Cigna, and UnitedHealthcare. We verify eligible co-pays prior to your arrival so you never face surprise outpatient invoices.
You may easily cancel or reschedule your slot through the "My Bookings" portal on this website or by phone up to 4 hours before your scheduled time without penalty.
Our encrypted telehealth appointments cover comprehensive history reviews, symptom evaluation, medication refills, and diagnostic ordering. If physical examination or specimen collection is indicated, the physician will seamlessly schedule an in-clinic follow-up.
While our Urgent Care triage welcomes acute walk-in patients during designated hours, we strongly recommend reserving a slot online to guarantee zero waiting room delay.
